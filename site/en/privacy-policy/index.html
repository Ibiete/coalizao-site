Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

1. Who we are

The Brazilian Coral Reef Coalition (Coalizão Corais do Brasil) is a Brazilian civil society initiative founded by Fundação Grupo Boticário and WWF-Brazil, with Bloom Ocean acting as Executive Secretariat. This policy explains how we handle personal data submitted through this website, in accordance with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018 — LGPD).

2. What data we collect

We only collect the data you voluntarily provide in the contact form: name, email, organization (optional), sector, type of participation, Working Groups of interest and your message. We do not collect sensitive data and ask that you do not include it in your message.

This website does not use tracking, advertising or behavioral analytics cookies. Fonts and images are served by the website itself.

3. How we use the data

  • To reply to your message and keep in touch about the subject you indicated;
  • To assess and follow up on expressions of interest in joining the Impact Network and the Working Groups;
  • To manage the Coalition's institutional relationships with interested organizations and people.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 7, I, LGPD), recorded when you submit the form.

4. Who we share it with

The data is received by the Coalition's Executive Secretariat and stored in a contracted customer relationship management (CRM) platform, with access restricted to the people responsible for responding. A copy of the message is sent by email to the Executive Secretariat. We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties for commercial purposes.

5. How long we keep it

We keep the data for as long as necessary for the purposes above or until you request its deletion, unless we are legally required to retain it.

6. Your rights

At any time, you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, portability or deletion of your data, and you may withdraw your consent (Art. 18, LGPD), by writing to coalizaocoraisdobrasil@gmail.com.

7. Ombudsman

Reports, suggestions and complaints about the Coalition may be sent to ouvidoria@wwf.org.br.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the website or to the law. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this page.
